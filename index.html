Drop in a photo, a video clip or a sound recording. The model names the aircraft type and model and shows exactly what it based that on. Everything runs in your browser; your files never leave your device.

Recognition switches on once the trained models are published to this site (Step 11 of the notebook). Until then you can explore the 3D aircraft and the hand-control simulation, or run the full app in Colab.

Or explore the hangar
Recognises passenger jets, fighter jets, helicopters and propeller aircraft. Drag the hologram to look around.
AERO-ID: explainable multimodal aircraft recognition. U-Net segmentation, ResNet-18 classifiers tuned by Particle Swarm Optimization, pseudo-labelling, CAM/Grad-CAM explanations and fine-grained aircraft-model recognition. Code on GitHub · Full app in Colab.
3D models: Boeing 737 AEW&C Prototype, General Dynamics F-16D Block 60 and SH-60B Seahawk Helicopter by Muhamad Mirza Arrafi (Sketchfab).
